A twin that pulls from low revs

The bodywork fades so you can see what it covers. Oil-cooled finned barrels, a 270° crank and a 689 cc capacity give 68 Nm at 6,000 rpm, so it accelerates hard without needing to be wrung out.

  • Ride-by-wire throttle with Road, Rain and Sport modes
  • 6-speed gearbox with a slip-and-assist clutch
  • 12,000 km between services

Steel trellis, adjustable suspension

A steel trellis frame carries a 41 mm upside-down fork and a single rear shock, both adjustable for preload and damping. Twin 298 mm discs with four-piston calipers do the stopping.

  • Wheelbase 1,410 mm for stable, quick steering
  • Rake 25° with a cast aluminium swingarm
  • Wheels 17 inch, 120/70 front and 180/55 rear

Upright, narrow and easy to reach the ground

The seat is 810 mm high and the 14 litre tank is cut in at the knees, so you can plant a foot at a stop and grip the bike with your legs on the move. The bar is 780 mm wide with the mirrors on short stalks.
